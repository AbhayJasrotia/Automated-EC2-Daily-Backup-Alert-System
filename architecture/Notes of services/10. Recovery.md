Failure & Recovery Testing
I tested both failure handling and actual backup recovery.

## Failure test

I temporarily changed the Lambda INSTANCE_ID to an invalid value.

The Lambda failed as expected.

The failure was recorded in CloudWatch Logs and SNS sent a failure notification.

Flow:

Invalid configuration
        ↓
Lambda failure
        ↓
CloudWatch Logs
        ↓
SNS FAILED notification

After testing, I restored the correct INSTANCE_ID.

## Successful recovery test

I selected a completed EBS snapshot created by the backup system.

I created a new EBS volume from the snapshot and attached it to the EC2 instance.

I mounted the recovered volume and verified that my backup test files were present.

Recovered files included:

- project-info.txt
- important-data.txt

## Recovery flow

EBS Snapshot
      ↓
New EBS Volume
      ↓
Attach to EC2
      ↓
Mount volume
      ↓
Verify recovered files

## What I learned

- Backup success does not automatically mean recovery has been tested.
- CloudWatch can be used to investigate Lambda failures.
- SNS can notify operators when a backup fails.
- EBS snapshots can be used to create new volumes for recovery.
- A recovery test helps verify that the backup contains usable data.

## Cleanup

After the recovery test, I detached and deleted the temporary recovery volume.
