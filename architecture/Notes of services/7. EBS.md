I automated the EC2 backup.

What I learned:
- EventBridge Scheduler can run tasks automatically on a schedule.
- I created a recurring daily schedule.
- The schedule invokes my Lambda function.
- Lambda creates an EBS snapshot.
- SNS sends the backup notification.

Schedule:
ec2-daily-backup-schedule
rate(1 day)

Final flow:

EventBridge Scheduler
        ↓
      Lambda
        ↓
   EBS Snapshot
        ↓
       SNS
        ↓
      Email

The backup no longer needs to be started manually.
